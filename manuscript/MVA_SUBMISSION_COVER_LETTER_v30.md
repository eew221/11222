# Cover Letter: Machine Vision and Applications

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, "Geometry-Defined Single-Owner PPE Evidence
Allocation and Cross-Worker Contamination Analysis," for publication in
*Machine Vision and Applications*.

The manuscript studies the decision layer between PPE object detections and
worker-specific safe, unsafe, or review outcomes in low-light tunnel scenes.
Its core contribution is a deterministic single-owner evidence representation
that prevents image-level PPE evidence from being copied to every worker. We
evaluate the representation through a four-role filename-group protocol,
explicit review-all fallback, geometry-defined reference analyses, and two
complementary blinded human-ownership audits. The second audit is a
pre-frozen source-stratified random open-candidate sample: three blind passes
produce 321 determinate PPE-owner rows from 66 images, on which RC-WSSI
matches 320 owner sets. We report this as sampled ownership evidence, not as
full-corpus semantic truth.

The paper deliberately limits its claims. Its Clopper--Pearson calculation is
reported as a model-conditional feasibility diagnostic under an idealized
independent worker-level model, not as a cluster-valid or deployment safety
guarantee. We also report the conservative reference-review-as-unsafe
sensitivity, group-level heterogeneity, and the distinction between
automatic-safe and end-to-end composite events. Global pooling is presented as
a mechanism-isolation baseline, not as a claim of universal superiority over
all association algorithms.

The work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been
approved by all listed authors. The public reproducibility repository provides
code, source, non-image manifests, blank audit templates, and aggregate audit
results under an MIT license. Original construction-scene images, per-image
labels, weights, and caches remain unavailable because of organization
confidentiality and privacy controls; the manuscript states this boundary and
offers editorial controlled-access discussion subject to authorization.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Yuge Lu, on behalf of all authors
